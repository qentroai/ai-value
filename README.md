# Find Your AI Opportunity

A 2-minute self-assessment by Qentro LLC. Visitors go from broad to specific: their goal, the part of the business, the exact problem, what it is worth, and whether AI is the right tool. They can shortlist up to three problems, ranked by value and AI fit, and download a PDF.

Runs entirely in the browser: no AI model, no sign-up. A copy of the results is emailed to Qentro only when the visitor leaves the "Send a copy" box checked.

Live at https://ai-value.qentrotech.com
